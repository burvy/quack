The port is in two places: `quack-app` in `tauri.conf.json` and `quack-ui` in `Trunk.toml`. It's a duck number for now, for ever actually.  
We only support two linkings being `rlib` and `cdylib`.  
`rlib` starts the program through `main.rs` and runs on desktops and `cdylib` uses the `run` function in the `lib.rs` and works for Android. 
I won't use `staticlib` which works for IOS because I need a Mac to make IOS apps and I use a windows laptop not a Mac. If you're reading
